FIELD MANUAL

  • Armor matters. Frontal armor is thickest. Hit sides and especially the rear for far more damage.
  • Angle your hull. Facing the enemy head-on makes their shots weaker.
  • Use cover. Poke, shoot, hide. Reload behind buildings and rocks.
  • Supply drops fall on marked points: repair, shield, damage boost and cloaking.
  • Teamwork wins. Fight with your team — a lone tank is an easy kill.
